List of trademark applications by Patrick Dufáe

From Dufáepedia, the free encyclopedia
🛈 This is a list of known trademark applications filed by Patrick Dufáe with the Canadian Intellectual Property Office. Most were rejected; a small number remain pending.

Beginning in 2019, Dufáe developed a habit — which he has called "a hobby, not a pathology"[1] — of filing trademark applications for concepts, sensations, and everyday phenomena he considered "unclaimed." The Canadian Intellectual Property Office has rejected the overwhelming majority of these filings, several with written responses that Dufáe has since framed and displayed in his home office.[1] The best-known of these, the 2022 attempt to trademark the colour beige, is covered in detail on the main Patrick Dufáe article.

Applications

Year filed Trademark sought Status Notes
2019 The numeral 7, used in isolation Rejected Dufáe argued 7 was "the only number with any personality."[1]
2020 The smell of rain on hot pavement Rejected Filing cited "petrichor" by name; examiners noted the scent could not be affixed to packaging.
2020 The phrase "Have a nice day" Rejected Widely reported as his first application to draw local press attention.[2]
2021 Tuesdays Withdrawn Withdrawn after Dufáe concluded Mondays were "the more marketable day."
2021 The left sock of a matching pair, sold separately Rejected Filed the same year he founded the World Sock-Puppetry Federation.[3]
2022 The colour beige Rejected His most publicized filing; read aloud in full on Radio-Canada. See main article.
2022 The sound of a sneeze Rejected Filed six weeks after the beige rejection, which Dufáe described as "a palate cleanser."[1]
2023 The taste of plain tap water Rejected Examiners' response reportedly consisted of a single question mark.[1]
2023 The word "um" as a spoken filler Pending Still under review as of the last update to this page.
2024 Eggshell (a shade of off-white) Rejected Widely read as a rematch of the beige filing; examiners cited the earlier decision directly.
2024 Four minutes and thirty-three seconds of silence Rejected Filing included a blank audio attachment.
2025 The concept of Mondays Pending Filed shortly after withdrawing the Tuesdays application.
